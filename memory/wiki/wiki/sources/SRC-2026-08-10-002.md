# 首页.md

## Summary

This source is the homepage (首页) of an Obsidian vault titled "考公错题本" (Civil Service Exam Wrong Question Notebook), built as a dashboard. It aggregates and displays data from subfolders of the vault — 错题 (wrong questions), 词语辨析 (word discrimination), 知识点 (knowledge points), and 模板 (templates) — using Dataview queries to show total counts, recent wrong questions, high-frequency error types, categorization by module, this week's wrong questions, a knowledge graph by tags, and a tag cloud.

The dashboard also documents an adaptive review system ("自适应复习系统") with six mastery levels — 新学 (new), 初识, 熟悉, 掌握, 熟练, 精通 — that adjust push frequency from every day up to every 30 days based on the number of correct answers (up to 5 correct answers to reach 精通). A usage method is described: the user receives flashcard pushes at 9 AM daily, replies "对" (correct) or "错了+你的理解" (wrong + their understanding), and MiMo (an AI) judges and updates the mastery level, adjusting future push frequency.

Finally, the page provides quick operation links to review views (今日复习, 复习看板), templates for creating new 行测 (administrative aptitude test) and 申论 (essay) wrong questions, the word discrimination library, and a weekly review template, plus tag-based navigation for the five 行测 modules (判断推理, 言语理解, 数量关系, 资料分析, 常识判断) and 申论.

## Key Takeaways

- The page is a Dataview-driven Obsidian dashboard that aggregates wrong questions, word discrimination entries, and knowledge points stored in vault subfolders.
- An adaptive review system uses a six-level mastery ladder (新学→初识→熟悉→掌握→熟练→精通) with push frequencies scaling from daily to every 30 days.
- Mastery level is determined by consecutive correct answers: 1 correct → 初识 (every 2 days), up to 5 correct → 精通 (every 30 days).
- Daily workflow: 9 AM flashcard push, user replies '对' or '错了+你的理解', and MiMo judges the answer and auto-updates the mastery level.
- Navigation is organized by exam module tags: 判断推理, 言语理解, 数量关系, 资料分析, 常识判断, and 申论.
- Quick-operation links exist for 今日复习, 复习看板, new-question templates (行测 and 申论), the 词语辨析 library, and 周复习 template.

## Contradictions

⚠️ **Contradiction**: The stated goal of spaced repetition is to increase intervals after successes, yet the table shows intervals only increasing with consecutive correct answers while claiming wrong answers trigger '加强推送' (stronger/more frequent pushing) — the exact penalty schedule for wrong answers is not defined in the table.
⚠️ **Contradiction**: The adaptive review table maps correct-answer counts (1–5) to levels, but the flow describes MiMo judging answers and updating levels without clarifying whether an incorrect answer resets the count or moves the learner down a level.
