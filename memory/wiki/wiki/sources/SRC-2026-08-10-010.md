# 资料分析-知识点体系.md

## Summary

This note is a comprehensive knowledge system for the "资料分析" (Data Analysis) section of the 行测 (Administrative Aptitude Test), used in Chinese civil service exam preparation. It organizes all exam points into a checklist framework that maps directly to question-solving steps, with a memorization rhyme (速查口诀) covering the core logic: distinguishing 现期/基期/间隔年 (current/base/interval periods), growth rates vs. increments, proportion/baseline/comparison problems judged by comparing a and b growth rates, and quick calculation methods.

The document details 16 basic concepts across three categories: time-related concepts (现期, 基期, 间隔基期, 年均增长基期), indicator types (流量/存量指标, 同比/环比, 顺差/逆差, 百分点, 成数/翻番), and statistical material reading rules (table/chart conventions, unit conversions, and the rule that all *-annotated notes must be read first). It then presents 18 growth formulas covering ordinary growth (基期量 = 现期量/(1+增长率) as the most common), special growth types (间隔增长率, 混合增长率, 年均增长率, 乘积增长率, 比值增长率), and growth comparison rules (大大则大, 一大一小看乘积).

The note also covers 12 ratio formulas (比重/平均数/倍数) including period-change judgments (a>b rise, a<b fall), the 倍数 special expression trap ("A是B的n倍" vs "A比B多n倍"), and derived formulas like 利润率 and 产销率. Finally, it lists 6 quick calculation techniques (估算法, 直除法, 特殊分数法, 分数比较, 尾数法, 截位修正) with their applicability conditions based on option gap size, and links to related notes on material classification, exam traps, and a wrong-question index.

## Key Takeaways

- 所有考点不可再拆分，直接对应每道考题的步骤，考前逐个自查打勾确认掌握
- 基期量 = 现期量/(1+增长率) 是最常见的增长类核心公式
- 间隔增长率 r = r₁ + r₂ + r₁×r₂；当 r₁、r₂ 均＜10% 时 r₁×r₂ 可忽略
- 增长量比较遵循「大大则大」和「一大一小看乘积」两条规则
- 比例升降判断看 a 与 b 的大小：a>b 上升、a<b 下降、a=b 不变；比重变化量一定小于 |a-b|
- 倍数表述易错点：「A是B的n倍」= A/B = n，而「A比B多n倍」= A/B = n+1
- 利润率在资料分析中口径为 利润总额/营业收入，而非成本利润率
- 速算技巧按选项差距选法：差距≥10%用估算法，<10%用直除法，<5%用截位修正
- 特殊分数法：增长率≈1/n 时增长量=现期/(n+1)，减少量=现期/(n-1)
- 所有 * 标注的统计注释必须先读，必有考点；人口增长率单位用 ‰（千分号）

## Contradictions

⚠️ **Contradiction**: 利润率在资料分析中采用 利润总额/营业收入 口径，与财务/成本口径的利润率（利润/成本）不同，使用时常被混淆
⚠️ **Contradiction**: 倍数表述中「A是B的n倍」与「A比B多n倍」含义不同（后者 A/B=n+1），是常见易错点
