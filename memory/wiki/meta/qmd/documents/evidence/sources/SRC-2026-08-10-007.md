# INDEX.md

## Summary

This source is the structured index (table of contents) of 《资料分析一本通》 (a 426-page Chinese civil service exam prep textbook on Data Analysis/资料分析), authored by 宋捏捏 and 怀瑾. It explicitly notes that this is the original textbook's structured index rather than a personal system, organized into six parts for easy lookup of knowledge points.

The book's six parts cover: (1) understanding the Data Analysis section including the exam outline (国考 4 articles/20 questions, provincial exams 3-4 articles) and proposition thinking; (2) basic terms and formulas such as 同比/环比, growth rate, growth amount, base/current period, proportion, average, and multiple; (3) the statistical system and how to read source materials; (4) a three-step question review method for basic and comprehensive question types; (5) speed calculation techniques and awareness-building including error analysis, addition/subtraction methods, multiplication/division methods, and comparison methods; and (6) classic question reviews and 500 practice questions.

The final part is detailed with 20 question models mapped to page numbers, covering base period calculation, growth amount and rate computation/comparison, interval and mixed growth rates, ratio (比值) categories, cross multiplication, growth contribution rate, and nominal vs. real growth. A "mother questions" compilation (母题汇总) breaks the 500 questions into eight sections (base period, current period, growth amount, growth rate, proportion, average, multiple, special test points) with counts and page numbers. The source also lists two related (yet-to-be-built) personal wiki notes for a personal problem-solving system and error book.

## Key Takeaways

- 《资料分析一本通》 is a 426-page textbook by 宋捏捏/怀瑾 structured as an indexed lookup reference for Data Analysis (资料分析) exam prep.
- The book is organized into six parts: exam overview, basic terms/formulas, statistical system & material reading, three-step question review, speed calculation techniques, and classic questions with 500 practice problems.
- The 20-model framework (二十大模型) systematically organizes all major Data Analysis question archetypes with page references, from base period calculation to nominal vs. real growth.
- Key speed-calculation techniques include error analysis, 尾数法, 高位叠加, 削峰填谷, 截位计算, 特殊值法, and 十字交叉法.
- The 母题汇总 (mother questions) assigns 500 practice questions across 8 sections: 基期(9), 现期(20), 增长量(17), 增长率(50), 比重(31), 平均数(12), 倍数(9), and 特殊考点(7).
- National exam (国考) format: 4 articles/20 questions; provincial exams typically 3-4 articles.
- The index distinguishes itself from a personal problem-solving system, pointing to separate wiki notes for that purpose.
